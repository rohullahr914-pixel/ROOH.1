# ROOH human avatar source

The anatomical head mesh is **Infinite, 3D Head Scan by Lee Perry-Smith**,
originally published by **Infinite-Realities**, based on work at
[triplegangers.com](https://www.triplegangers.com/).

The model and accompanying normal map are distributed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The upstream license is preserved verbatim in `LeePerrySmith_License.txt`.

## Pinned upstream files

These files were copied from the official Three.js repository at tag **r176**:

- [LeePerrySmith.glb](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/r176/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith/LeePerrySmith.glb)
- [Infinite-Level_02_Tangent_SmoothUV.jpg](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/r176/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith/Infinite-Level_02_Tangent_SmoothUV.jpg)
- [LeePerrySmith_License.txt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/r176/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith/LeePerrySmith_License.txt)

## Rendering adaptation

The downloaded source files are unchanged. ROOH adapts their presentation at
runtime with its own scale, positioning, lighting, materials and holographic
effects. These rendering changes are ROOH's work, not part of the original scan.
The scan creator does not endorse ROOH.
